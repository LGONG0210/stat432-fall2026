title: "When Is Ultra Reasoning Worth It?"
author: "Lu Gong (lugong2)"
---

I have used coding agents to generate review materials and practice exams, and to read and organize course syllabi. These tasks vary in complexity, but I often wonder whether I should keep the agent at Ultra, the highest reasoning-effort setting, or adjust it for each task.

My current hypothesis is that Ultra provides the highest ceiling for task completion, especially for tasks that require synthesis and organization. For simple and repetitive tasks, lower reasoning may be sufficient while using fewer tokens. However, I have not measured how many tokens it actually saves or how much the completeness of the result changes.

How large is the actual trade-off between result completeness and token use across reasoning levels? Is there a principled way to decide when Ultra is worthwhile and when a lower setting is enough?
