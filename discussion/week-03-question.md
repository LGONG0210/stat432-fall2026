title: "Why can ridge predict better with a higher training SSE?"

OLS minimizes the training SSE, but a low training error may come from overfitting noise. How can ridge regression reduce expected test error even though its training SSE is higher? In terms of the bias-variance trade-off, what needs to happen for ridge to improve prediction, and why might too much shrinkage make prediction worse?
